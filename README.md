## 🚀 Things I've built

- **[Graduate Better](https://github.com/seantan02/graduateBetter)** (a.k.a. Graduate Badger 🦡) · Founder · *Sep 2023 – May 2025*<br>
  A degree optimizer that finds UW–Madison students the shortest path to graduation, even with multiple majors. Students pick their majors and enter their courses, and a heavily modified A\* search, tuned with priority queues and pass-by-reference data handling, builds the plan. My friend Anton helped brainstorm the core algorithm. The site is shut down now; the repo is a public sample of the private codebase.<br>
  `C#` `ASP.NET Core` `React` `TypeScript` `Vite` `Python` `Airflow` `Docker` `Google Cloud`

- **[Error Log Solution Finder](https://github.com/seantan02/summary_AI)** · *May 2024 – Sep 2024*<br>
  A summarization model I trained from scratch in PyTorch (Transformer or Seq2Seq with attention) on 10,000 rows of log data. It shortens error logs by about 25%, then searches Google with the summary and pulls the best fix from StackOverflow.<br>
  `Python` `PyTorch` `pandas` `Transformer` `Seq2Seq`

- **[Data Analysis](https://github.com/seantan02/data-analysis)**<br>
  Generates HTML reports that compare old and new test results with distribution plots, QQ-plots, t-tests and permutation tests. It also projects trends by fitting polynomial (degree 1–5) and negative-exponential curves and keeping the one with the lowest MSE. Modeled on what I built at my SKYGEN internship.<br>
  `Python` `scikit-learn` `Statistics` `InfluxDB`

- **[Task Manager](https://github.com/seantan02/Task-Manager)** · *Dec 2024 – Feb 2025*<br>
